A rule matches mail to its real recipient in its inbox — or, for “any inbox”, in every inbox, each with its own address format (e.g. any@example.com matches team1.any~example.com@{{$d.Domain}} in a {inbox}.{local}~{domain} inbox). Overlapping rules forward once per destination. A catch-all pattern on your domain (team1.*@{{$d.Domain}}) also works.

Incoming mail that matches a rule is forwarded by the Worker as it arrives — also while the app is offline. Replies from the destination go straight to the original sender. Destinations must be verified addresses.
